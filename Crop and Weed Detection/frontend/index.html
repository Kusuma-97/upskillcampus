False-positive filter pipeline

Every crop detection passes through these gates before it can be labeled diseased — this is what stops dead matter and debris piles from being misread as disease.

How this works

  • ▸A YOLO11n detector (trained on the 1,300-image sesame crop/weed dataset) localizes crop and weed regions.
  • ▸An ExG vegetation gate, a geometry check, and weed-cluster suppression filter out dead matter and debris before disease scoring.
  • ▸Each surviving crop region is passed to a lightweight disease classifier.
  • ▸All of this runs CPU-only — no GPU required on the farm.
